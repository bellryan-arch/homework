What a daily session looks like

1. Choose a level

Start at a grade from 1–8 or use the current level chosen by the parent.

2. Pick the workload

Choose 10, 25, 40, 60 or 80 questions depending on the time available.

3. Focus the practice

Use adaptive, mixed or a specific skill such as multiplication, fractions, decimals, percents or word problems.

4. Fix mistakes

Wrong answers stay visible as needing correction so the learner can finish the thinking rather than just accept a score.

Why short daily practice works better for this tool

The goal is consistency. A parent can use a small question set on busy days and a longer page when there is more time. Because the tool records recent pages and skill mastery in the browser, daily work becomes visible without turning the routine into a complicated learning-management system.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to use it every day?

No. "Daily" is a suggested routine, not a requirement. Use it as often as fits your family or classroom.

Can I choose a specific skill?

Yes. The focus menu includes adaptive, mixed, word problems, addition/subtraction, multiplication/division, decimals, fractions, percents and order of operations.
