Questions parents ask

English Practice FAQ

Does Bell's English require an account?+

No. It works in the browser and saves progress locally on this device.

Can I enter a school spelling list?+

Yes. Choose Spelling Studio, paste the words, and use Listen for a device-read spelling prompt.

What happens after a wrong answer?+

The item stays marked and editable. The learner must correct it before the learning quest is complete.

How does Chore Quest connect?+

Finishing all corrections creates a Bell Pass with a modest XP reward. Chore Quest can import it for the selected child and rejects duplicate passes.

Is this fully aligned to the Ontario curriculum?+

The practice supports common Grade 3–6 language foundations, but it is not a complete curriculum or a claim of full curriculum coverage.
