Plain-language privacy

Practice stays on this device.

Bell's English is designed to work without a student account.

What is stored

Completed-session history, first-try scores, selected child slot and custom spelling words are stored in this browser using local storage. They are not automatically synchronized to another device.

Chore Quest rewards

After every answer is corrected, Bell's English creates a small Bell Pass containing the learning tool, activity, grade, score, selected child slot, reward amount, time and a random ID. The pass is stored for up to 48 hours in a cookie available to BellStuff subdomains and may also be included in the Chore Quest link. It does not contain a child's name, answer text or custom spelling words.

Connected family names

If you choose Connect learning in Chore Quest, a 30-day cookie shares up to four child names among BellStuff subdomains on this browser. You can disconnect names in Chore Quest at any time. The names are separate from the reward pass and practice history.

Delete or back up

Open the Parent view to download an English-practice backup or clear English data from this browser. Chore Quest keeps its own local data and reward-import ledger.

What this first version does not do

There is no cloud student profile, advertising tracker or public leaderboard in this build. Browser storage can be erased by clearing site data.
