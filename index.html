Questions parents ask

Math Practice FAQ

Does Math Practice require an account?+

No. This single-child browser version does not require a student account. Progress is saved locally in this browser.

Can I print the practice pages?+

Yes. Use the Print button to turn the current practice page into a paper-based worksheet.

What happens when an answer is wrong?+

Incorrect answers are marked as needing correction so the learner can fix mistakes instead of only seeing a final score.

Can a parent control progression?+

Yes. Parent settings include solution access, mastery and completion thresholds, level controls and automatic advancement.

Is this a full math curriculum?+

No. Bell's Math Practice is designed for focused practice and reinforcement alongside school or homeschool learning.
